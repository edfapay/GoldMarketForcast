## 5. Oil–gold relationship
Two channels compete: *oil↑→inflation↑→yields↑→gold↓* vs. *geopolitical risk↑→safe-haven→gold↑*. **The safe-haven channel currently dominates** — gold is firm despite the 10Y at a 3-year high. The inflation/yield channel is the *delayed* threat, working **through the Fed** (a hawkish Sep 16 is how high oil could ultimately bite gold).
